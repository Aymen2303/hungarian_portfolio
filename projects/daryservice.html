Project overview

Developed as a startup and master's thesis project, DaryService brings people looking for home repairs together with workers and brokers. Customers can explore services such as electrical work, air-conditioning repair, and plumbing, then arrange an appointment that suits them.

The platform also supports broker-led coordination: brokers receive appointment opportunities and can accept or cancel them. Both sides are notified when an appointment action is taken.

01

For customers

Find workers and handymen across services such as electrical work, air-conditioning, and plumbing. Customers can set a preferred date and time when requesting an appointment.

02

Find a broker

Customers can browse brokers themselves. In some cases, the platform suggests brokers with ratings above 3.5 stars to help customers choose a service contact.

03

For brokers

Brokers receive suggested appointments and can choose to accept or cancel each opportunity, helping them manage incoming service requests.

04

Appointment updates

Notifications keep both parties informed when an appointment action is taken, so customers and brokers stay up to date on the request.

THE SERVICE JOURNEY

DaryService brings service discovery, appointment scheduling, broker decisions, and timely notifications into one coordinated home-services experience.
