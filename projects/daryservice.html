Project overview

DaryService was developed as a startup and master's thesis project. It connects people looking for home repairs with workers and brokers, bringing service discovery and appointment coordination into one platform.

Customers can browse services such as electrical work, air-conditioning, and plumbing, select a date and time, and request an appointment. They can look for brokers themselves, or receive broker suggestions from the platform.

01

Find a service

People can look for workers and handymen across home-service categories, including electrical work, air-conditioning, and plumbing.

02

Schedule an appointment

Customers choose a preferred date and time when requesting a service appointment.

03

Choose a broker

Customers may browse brokers manually. The platform can also suggest brokers rated above 3.5 stars.

04

Coordinate the request

Brokers receive suggested appointments and can accept or cancel them. Notifications keep both parties informed when an appointment action is taken.

THE SERVICE JOURNEY

From finding help to appointment coordination.

DaryService brings service discovery, scheduling, broker decisions, and notifications together to make the process clearer for customers and brokers.
